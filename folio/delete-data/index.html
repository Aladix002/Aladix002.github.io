Folio: delete your data

Folio is developed by Filip Botlo. This page explains how to delete the data that Folio stores or sends.

Data on your phone

Almost everything Folio stores stays on your phone: your documents and their text, highlights, review history, reading position, streak, gate history and settings. We never receive it.

To delete it:

  • In the app, open Settings > Privacy > Delete all my data. This deletes all app data on your phone at once.
  • Or uninstall Folio. Android deletes all app data with it.

To delete a single document, open it in the Library and choose Delete. This deletes its file, text, cards and highlights. You can do this even without a subscription.

Usage statistics and crash reports

These are sent only if you turned them on in Settings > Privacy. To stop them, turn them off: the app stops sending at once and deletes the random ID and unsent events on your phone.

Data already sent is anonymous and kept at most 12 months (usage statistics, PostHog, EU) and at most 90 days (crash reports, Sentry, EU), then deleted automatically. To have it deleted sooner, write to filip.botlo@gmail.com with the subject "Folio data deletion" and, if you can, your device model and the approximate dates you used the app. We answer within one month.

Subscription

Payments and purchase history are handled by Google Play. To cancel a subscription, use Google Play > Payments and subscriptions. To delete purchase data, see the Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

What is not deleted

Nothing is kept by us after the steps above. Google may keep purchase records as required by law.

Full privacy policy: https://aladix002.github.io/folio/privacy/
