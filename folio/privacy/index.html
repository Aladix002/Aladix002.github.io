Folio privacy policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Folio is an app for reading your own documents (PDF, EPUB, text, Markdown and photos of pages) as short cards, with highlights that come back for review and an optional gate before apps you choose. It works on your phone, without an account and without a server of ours.

1. Who we are

Controller: Filip Botlo, Slovakia, filip.botlo@gmail.com. We have not appointed a data protection officer, as it is not required for us.

2. Summary

  • No account. No ads. No advertising or data-broker SDKs. We run no server that receives your data.
  • Your documents never leave your phone through the app. Their files, names, titles and text are not sent to us or anyone else, and are not included in usage statistics, crash reports, logs or Android backups.
  • Text recognition (OCR) runs on your phone. For some scripts, Google Play services downloads a recognition model (section 5).
  • Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports are off by default. They are sent only if you turn them on, and only if you confirm you are 16 or older.
  • The app does not use your location, contacts, camera permission or access to all files.

We have no access to this data.

How documents get in. You choose files with the Android file picker, share them to Folio from another app, or take a photo with your camera app. Folio copies each document into its private storage on your phone. It does not ask for access to all your files or photos.

Backups. Your documents, their text, highlights and review history are kept in separate files that are never included in Android backups: not in your Google backup and not in a transfer to a new phone. Other app data (saved cards, streak, daily sets, settings) may be included in your own Google backup or a transfer to a new phone, on Android 9 and newer and only if the backup can be encrypted. This is between you and Google; we cannot read it. You can turn backup off in Android settings.

Deletion. Deleting a document in Folio deletes its file, text, cards and highlights. Settings > Privacy > "Delete all my data" deletes everything. Uninstalling the app also deletes it.

4. Data sent only with your consent

You give consent separately for each of the two purposes below (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time in Settings > Privacy, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal does not affect processing before it.

4.1 Anonymous usage statistics (PostHog)

  • What: events such as "onboarding step finished", "screen viewed" (screen name only), "permission granted or not", "daily set started/completed" with the number of cards and duration, "apps chosen for the gate" (the number only); app version, a short library version code, app language, app edition; a random ID created by the analytics library.
  • Never: your documents, their names, titles or text, your highlights, what you search for, your intention, which other apps you use or gate, your name or e-mail.
  • No person profiles, no session recording, no automatic tap tracking, location lookup from IP turned off.
  • Processor: PostHog Inc., EU Cloud (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • When you turn it off, sending stops at once and the random ID and unsent events are deleted from your phone.

4.2 Crash reports (Sentry)

  • What: technical details of an error (error type and stack trace, app version, Android version, device model), whether the app session ended normally, and a short technical log filtered to remove e-mails, web addresses and app names; a random installation ID. Logs of the import only contain the file type, duration and an error code.
  • Never: your documents, their names or text, screenshots or screen recordings, an IP-based user ID.
  • Processor: Functional Software Inc. (Sentry), EU data region (Germany).
  • When you turn it off, reporting stops and unsent reports are deleted from your phone.

4.3 Age

You can give these consents only if you confirm that you are 16 or older. Otherwise both stay off.

4.4 Retention

PostHog events: at most 12 months. Sentry events: at most 90 days. Then deleted.

5. Text recognition, ML Kit and Google Play services

Folio recognizes text in photos of pages and scanned PDFs with Google ML Kit, on your phone. Images and recognized text are processed on the device and are not sent to us or to Google by Folio.

  • Latin script (English and most European languages): the model is part of the app and works offline.
  • Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Devanagari: the model is not part of the app. The first time you choose one of these scripts, Folio asks Google Play services on your phone to download it. The download is done by Google Play services, not by Folio.
  • ML Kit inside Folio is configured not to send its usage metrics: Folio removes the component ML Kit uses for that. Google Play services is a separate part of Android operated by Google. When it downloads or runs these models it may send Google diagnostic and usage information (for example device model, Android and Play services version, which model was requested, performance and errors), under Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). It does not receive your images or their text from Folio.

If Google Play services is missing, only Latin script can be recognized.

6. Other network requests

  • Folio does not download content and has no server. The sample text included in the app is part of the app.
  • Links to licenses, this policy or the feedback form open in your browser and are governed by those sites' policies.
  • Subscription (Google Play). After a 14-day free trial the app offers an optional subscription. Payment, purchase history and cancellation are handled entirely by Google Play under Google's privacy policy; we never receive your payment details. The app asks Google Play on your phone whether a subscription is active and remembers only the answer, the time of the last check and when you first opened the app (for the trial). No purchase token or order number is sent to us or stored in logs or analytics. Without a subscription you can still see and delete your documents and delete all your data in Settings.
  • Report a problem. The app shows you the full text of the report and you send it yourself (for example by e-mail). The app sends nothing on its own.

7. Permissions

  • Notifications: an optional morning reminder. It is approximate; the app does not use exact alarms.
  • Start at boot: to restore the reminder (and the gate, if it is on) after the phone restarts.
  • Edition with the app gate only: Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS), Display over other apps and a foreground service. They are used only on your phone to notice when you open an app you chose and to show the gate before it. The app checks usage only to compare the app on screen with the apps you chose. It keeps a local history of gate sessions and unlocks for your progress; it stays on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, access to all files or photos, accessibility services or a list of all installed apps.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Because we only hold random IDs, we may need the ID from your phone to find your data. Write to filip.botlo@gmail.com. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority, in Slovakia: Urad na ochranu osobnych udajov SR, Hranicna 12, 820 07 Bratislava, https://dataprotection.gov.sk.

9. Children

Folio is not directed at children under 13. Usage statistics and crash reports are available only to users who confirm they are 16 or older.

10. Security

All network traffic of the app (only the optional statistics and crash reports) uses HTTPS. Your documents and other local data are protected by Android app sandboxing.

11. Your documents and copyright

You are responsible for the documents you add. Folio only shows them to you on your phone and does not publish or share them. When you share a card yourself (as an image or text), it goes only where you send it.

12. Changes

We will publish changes here and update the effective date. If a change needs new consent, the app will ask you again.
